Help and transparency

Know what to expect.

Is ReyOS a rolling release?

ReyOS is based on Arch, with a tested snapshot channel. The repository documents its update strategy.

What hardware is supported?

Check release notes and test the live environment before installing. Hardware compatibility and performance vary.
