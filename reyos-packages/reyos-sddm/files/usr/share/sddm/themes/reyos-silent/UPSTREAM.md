SilentSDDM, restyled for ReyOS
===============================

Bundled from upstream for ReyOS's login screen, keeping its components/
Main.qml/fonts/icons unmodified. Only `configs/reyos.conf` is ReyOS's own
(based on upstream's config format, not copied from any one preset) and
`backgrounds/reyos.jpg` is ReyOS's own wallpaper. The stock preset configs
and background videos/images that shipped upstream are not included.
